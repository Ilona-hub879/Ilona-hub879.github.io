# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last updated:** July 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the self-employed person Ilona Samoviča (hereinafter referred to as the **"Data Controller"**) processes the personal data of visitors and clients on the website [https://www.prosolvely.com](https://www.prosolvely.com/) (hereinafter referred to as the **"Website"**), associated Telegram chatbots (hereinafter referred to as **"Chatbots"**), and WhatsApp communication channels.

### 1\. LEGAL BASIS AND PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

The Data Controller processes personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), based on the following legal grounds:

- **Consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)):** To respond to your inquiries submitted via email or the Website, or when you voluntarily start Chatbots or send messages via WhatsApp.
- **Contractual Necessity (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)):** To perform a contract or take pre-contractual steps at your request when ordering paid services.
- **Legal Obligation (GDPR Article 6(1)(c)):** To comply with statutory obligations under the laws of the Republic of Latvia (e.g., accounting and bookkeeping requirements).
- **Legitimate Interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)):** To ensure the security of the Website and maintain the proper technical functioning of our communication channels.

Your personal data is **not** subject to automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects you.

### 2\. CATEGORIES OF DATA PROCESSED

Depending on your interaction with us, we may process the following types of data:

- **Technical Data:** IP address, browser type, and session cookies collected by our hosting infrastructure.
- **Telegram Profile Data:** Telegram User ID, full name, username (@username), and message content.
- **WhatsApp Profile Data:** Phone number, WhatsApp profile name, and message content.
- **Communication Data:** Name, email address, and message content provided voluntarily by the user.
- **Payment and Customer Data:** Name, surname, and payment details required when ordering services.

### 3\. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES AND DATA TRANSFERS

To deliver our services, we use the following third-party service providers:

- **GitHub (GitHub Pages):** For hosting and storing Website code.
- **Telegram Messenger (Telegram FZ-LLC / Telegram Inc.):** As a platform for running Chatbots.
- **WhatsApp (WhatsApp Ireland Limited / Meta Platforms, Inc.):** As a communication channel. WhatsApp processes data in accordance with its own Privacy Policy.
- **Payment Institutions:** When making payments, data is transmitted directly to licensed financial institutions.

We rely on service providers, such as WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.) and Telegram (Telegram FZ-LLC), whose servers may be located outside the European Union / European Economic Area (EU/EEA), including in the United States. In such cases, data transfers are conducted on the basis of appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, to ensure a level of data protection equivalent to that of the EU. For more information regarding these transfer mechanisms, please contact us.

The Data Controller guarantees that personal data and message history collected from users are **not** used to train artificial intelligence (AI) models.

### 4\. TARGET AUDIENCE AND AGE RESTRICTIONS

The Website, Chatbots, and communication channels are intended solely for individuals who are at least 18 years old.

Our services are not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect or process personal data from children under 13. If you are between 13 and 18 years of age, please use our services only with the permission of a parent or legal guardian.

### 5\. YOUR RIGHTS AND CONTACT INFORMATION

You have the right to request access to, rectification, or erasure of your personal data, restrict or object to its processing (especially when based on legitimate interests), and request data portability. You also have the right to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

- **Contact Email:** info@prosolvely.com

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority — the Data State Inspectorate of Latvia (_Datu valsts inspekcija_, [www.dvi.gov.lv](https://www.dvi.gov.lv/)).

### 6\. DATA RETENTION PERIOD

Your personal data will be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected:

- **Communication Data (emails, messages):** Retained for 1 year from the date of the last contact.
- **Contractual Data:** Retained for the duration of the contract and until the expiration of statutory limitation periods.
- **Accounting Documents:** Retained for the periods specified under the Accounting Law of the Republic of Latvia.
