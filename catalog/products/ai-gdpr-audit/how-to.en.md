# AI GDPR Auditor — How to receive your full audit

**Thank you for your interest in our service.**

This product is **not a downloadable report file**. Documents you upload are **not stored permanently** and are **not written to the Operator's databases**. Document content is processed in real time in volatile memory, and the file is **fully deleted immediately after the analysis and report are complete**. You receive a **personal GDPR audit** for your documents in our application and a **link to it at your email address**. **Documents must be uploaded before payment.**

---

## What you get

- **A full AI GDPR audit of your document** (PDF or DOCX, **max 15 pages**, **max 1 MB**)
- Findings with risk levels, legal references (GDPR + selected national law), and rewrite suggestions
- A **private report link** by email after payment is confirmed

## How to get your audit (follow this order)

### Step 1 — Open our application

**{{app.url}}**

### Step 2 — Upload your document and enter the required details

1. Choose your **interface language**
2. Select **country / national law**
3. Enter your **email address** (use the same address at checkout)
4. Upload **PDF or DOCX** (maximum **15 pages** and **1 MB**)
5. Click **Check document for free (demo)** and wait for the preview

### Step 3 — After the demo appears, click a payment button

- **For a one-time check:** click **Unlock full audit — {{price.oneTime}}**
- **For a subscription:** click **Buy subscription for 30 documents — {{price.subscription}}/month**

A **payment provider checkout page** will open.

**Important:** Payment must be started from a button on the website **after** uploading your file. **Do not** buy the product separately in the payment provider's store **before** Step 2.

## After payment

- Your browser may automatically open the **report page** (heading **GDPR Audit Report**)
- You will receive an email with subject **"Your AI GDPR Audit report is ready"** — use the **Open full report** button in that email
- If you have an active **subscription**, you can upload and check further documents within your limit (**up to 30 documents per month**) using the **same email**
- Analysis usually takes a few minutes; scanned PDFs may take longer
- We do **not** keep your original file permanently — be sure to save your report

## Problems?

| Issue | What to do |
|-------|------------|
| No report after payment | Wait 5–15 minutes, check spam, open the email and click **Open full report** |
| Paid via payment provider before uploading | Contact support with your order number and email |
| **Report not found** | Use the **Open full report** button in our email |
| PDF cannot be read | Convert the file via **https://smallpdf.com** and upload again |
| Document too long | Up to **15 pages / 1 MB** per file. Split the document if needed |
| Wrong document | **One-time payment:** one payment = one document. A new file requires a new purchase. **Subscription:** you can upload a new file; it uses 1 of your 30 available checks |
| Subscription limit reached | The 30-document limit renews monthly when the subscription renews. If you run out early, buy a one-time check for {{price.oneTime}} or wait for renewal |
